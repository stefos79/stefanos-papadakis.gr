Why it matters

The role of bonds in your portfolio

Bonds provide stability against the swings of equity markets. In Accelerator Plus, strategies such as NN Hellas Conservative (at least 80% in bonds/cash) and NN Hellas Global Bonds are aimed at investors who want exposure to global markets with lower volatility, while the more equity-oriented strategies (Balanced, Appreciation) use bonds as a “counterweight” to equities.
