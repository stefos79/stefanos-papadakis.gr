Services for businesses

Your business, shielded against the unexpected.

A business isn’t only at risk from a fire or a theft. It is also at risk when it loses a key person, when a partner leaves or when a third-party claim arrives without warning. Identifying and analysing insurable risks is now essential for smooth operations and a stable future.

01

Key Person insurance

Every business has people whose absence would bring it to a halt. Key Person insurance provides immediate liquidity if a key person dies or becomes unable to work, so the business can bridge the gap, find a replacement and carry on without being shaken.

Read more →
02

Partner buy-sell agreements

What happens to a partner’s share if they leave unexpectedly? With the right cover, the funding for the buy-out is secured in advance, at a price that is fair to everyone. The remaining partners keep control of the company and the heirs receive the value that is due to them.

Read more →
03

Tax-efficient planning

The current tax framework offers incentives for certain corporate benefits and programmes. Working with your accountant, we identify the financial tools that make use of them, so protecting your business and its people also works as a tax advantage.

Read more →
04

Business debt protection

Loans and obligations don’t stop if something happens to the owner or a key person. The right cover protects creditors and provides immediate liquidity, so the family and the business aren’t burdened with debt at the worst possible moment.

Read more →
05

Business continuity & succession

A business built with hard work shouldn’t end when its founder steps away. We plan the handover to the next generation or new owners in good time, with the necessary liquidity and clear rules, so the change happens smoothly.

Read more →
06

Group health insurance for staff

Good people stay where they feel cared for. A group health or pension plan gives your staff benefits beyond the public system. It builds loyalty, helps you attract talent and is often more efficient than an equivalent pay rise.

Read more →
07

Business liability

An accident on your premises, a faulty product or a mistake in your services can lead to claims beyond what the business can absorb. We cover your liability towards third parties and employees, according to the nature of your work.

Read more →
08

Company property & fleet

Buildings, stock, equipment and vehicles are the capital that keeps the business standing. Every year we review the market to find the best balance of cover and cost, with special rates for businesses and vehicle fleets.

Read more →
09

Cyber risk & GDPR

A cyber attack or a data breach can halt your operations, bring fines and damage your reputation. Cyber insurance covers the loss, the cost of recovery and third-party claims, in a world that moves digitally and fast.
