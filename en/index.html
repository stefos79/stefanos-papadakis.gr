The method

First I listen. Then I advise.

Every recommendation starts from your own goals and the risks you actually face. I follow the Financial Planning methodology, so every decision rests on numbers, not impressions.

01

Needs analysis

Together we map out your financial picture: income, obligations, existing policies and goals. We spot the gaps, what isn’t covered today and what you are paying for without needing it.

02

Strategy & solutions

I design a long-term protection and investment plan tailored to your budget. I explain in plain words what each solution covers, what it costs and why I recommend it, so you can decide with a clear picture.

03

Ongoing review

Life changes: marriage, children, a new business, retirement. We review your plan regularly and at every major change, so it stays relevant. And when a claim needs to be made, I’m by your side throughout the process.

About me

Stefanos Papadakis

I studied in the United Kingdom, earning a BSc in Information Management Systems from the University of Essex and an MSc in E-Commerce from the University of Plymouth.

For more than 15 years I have worked in insurance and financial services. I have worked with the largest insurance companies in Greece, designed wealth management strategies for high-net-worth individuals and set up advanced insurance programmes for businesses.

Today I am a Senior Insurance Agent at NN Hellas and a certified Financial Planner from Achmea Academy. My approach always starts with an in-depth analysis of each client’s needs and leads to long-term strategies, with investment plans aimed at stability and growth.

A loyal client base and many referrals from industry colleagues are the greatest recognition of my work. My goal remains dedicated service, continuous development and the financial well-being of my clients.

I was born and work in Chania, and most of my clients come to me through referrals. To me, that means every relationship is a personal promise: to be available, to speak plainly and to be there when it really matters, at the moment of a loss or a claim.
